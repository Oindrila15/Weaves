# Open questions

## Decided (7 October 2026)

- **Coverage:** all handloom weaves in India, from information available online, stored in a knowledge repository that the chatbot searches (RAG).
- **Language:** English only.
- **Success:** footfall and AI quality, not weavers onboarded. Starter targets chosen by Claude: [success-targets.md](../knowledge/policies/success-targets.md).
- **Weaver listing:** public profile link only, no outreach.
- **Influencers:** Oindrila will ask them once the product is ready.
- **Cost to users:** free.
- **Budget:** no more than $50 per month in total: [budget.md](../knowledge/policies/budget.md).
- **Form factor:** website first, working well on phones; installable app later.
- **Exam guide:** downloaded and read; domains recorded in `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Sources:** any public source, in five trust tiers: [source-tiers-and-trust.md](../knowledge/policies/source-tiers-and-trust.md).
- **Meta (Instagram and Facebook):** no automated collection; links added by hand: [meta-platforms.md](../knowledge/policies/meta-platforms.md).
- **Search tool and web content:** Claude API web search, summaries only, credit and link: [web-search-and-content-use.md](../knowledge/policies/web-search-and-content-use.md).
- **Photos:** public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA only: [photos-and-licences.md](../knowledge/policies/photos-and-licences.md).
- **Maps:** India's outer boundary from Survey of India material: [maps.md](../knowledge/policies/maps.md).
- **Contacts:** no copying of phone numbers or emails; listing needs consent: [personal-data-and-contacts.md](../knowledge/policies/personal-data-and-contacts.md).
- **Chatbot scope:** [chatbot-scope.md](../knowledge/policies/chatbot-scope.md).

## Still open

1. **Legal check before launch.** A lawyer should review listing named individuals, the "Know a weaver?" form, how much we may summarise from other sites, and map compliance, and community photo uploads (photos of people, licence wording).
2. **Government pages.** Open and check each official page by hand (the handlooms portal could not be opened by our tool) and confirm the count of GI handloom weaves.
3. **Meta checks.** Confirm the hashtag search details on Meta's own page, and check Business Discovery, Facebook Page access and post embedding, before Phase 2 or 3.
4. **Hosting prices.** Price hosting, a domain and a map service when we choose the tools.
5. **Spend limit.** Confirm that the Anthropic Console can set a monthly spend limit on Oindrila's account.
