# Open questions

## Decided (7 October 2026)

- **Coverage:** all handloom weaves in India, from information available online, stored in a knowledge repository that the chatbot searches (RAG).
- **Language:** English only.
- **Success:** measured by footfall and AI quality, not by weavers onboarded.
- **Weaver listing:** public profile link only, no outreach.
- **Influencers:** Oindrila will ask them once the product is ready.
- **Cost to users:** free.
- **Form factor:** website first, working well on phones; installable app later.
- **Budget:** no more than $50 per month in total (AI usage and hosting).
- **Exam guide:** downloaded and read; domains recorded in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Still open

1. **Targets.** What numbers count as success for the website and AI measures in the BRD?
2. **Government sources.** Which official pages and datasets are usable? To be checked at the start of the build.
3. **Meta's rules.** What do Instagram's and Facebook's current developer terms allow?
4. **Search tool terms.** What do the terms of the web search tool the discovery agent will use allow?
5. **Photos.** Which open-licence sources do we use (for example Wikimedia Commons), and how do we record licence and credit?
6. **Map data.** Which approved source gives correct India boundaries, and what do Indian map rules require?
7. **Public contact details.** When a weaver posts their own number publicly, may we copy it? Get a lawyer's view before launch.
8. **Chatbot scope.** Which questions should it answer, and which should it decline?
9. **Web content use.** How much can we summarise from other websites, and how do we credit them?
