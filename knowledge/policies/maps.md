# Maps of India

**Last checked:** 7 October 2026 · **Review when:** before launch (legal check), or when the guidelines change.

## What we found

- India's 2021 geospatial guidelines removed most approval and licence requirements for making and publishing maps and replaced them with self-certification. [Summary](https://nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/technology-law-analysis/maps-and-geospatial-data-in-india-regime-liberalized-4678)
- The outer boundary of political maps must match the one published by the Survey of India. [OpenStreetMap wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/India/Government_of_India)
- Wrong maps of India can carry legal penalties. We did not confirm the details.

## Weaves policy

1. The outer boundary of India comes from Survey of India material. We do not use the default boundary of a map service such as OpenStreetMap or Google.
2. States and regions are simple clickable shapes drawn inside that boundary.
3. We keep a note of where the boundary data came from, its licence and the date.
4. We self-certify compliance with the guidelines before launch. **Legal check before launch.**
5. Weave regions follow tradition (for example Kancheepuram), not always state lines, so each weave page also names its state.
