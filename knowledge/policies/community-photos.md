# Community photos

**Last checked:** 7 October 2026 · **Review when:** before this feature is built, before launch (legal check), or after any complaint. Not legal advice.

Visitors can upload photos of themselves wearing sarees, or of sarees of a particular weave. This sits next to "Know a weaver?".

## Rules for uploaders (shown on the upload form)

1. You took the photo, or you own the rights to it.
2. If a person appears, it is you, or the person has agreed. **No photos of strangers and none of children.**
3. You tell us which weave the saree is, and say how you know (bought from a weaver, a shop, inherited, not sure).
4. You allow Weaves to show the photo on the site and credit you with the name you choose, or as "Anonymous". This licence is non-exclusive. You keep the rights.
5. You can ask us to delete your photo at any time.

## What Weaves does

- **Label:** every photo shows "Community photo, not verified". It is Tier 5 in [source-tiers-and-trust.md](source-tiers-and-trust.md) and never proof that a saree is genuine or belongs to a weave.
- **Review before publishing:** an automated check (Claude reading the image) flags unsuitable, unrelated or people-heavy photos, and a person approves every photo before it appears.
- **Privacy:** location and camera details (EXIF data) are removed on upload. No names or contact details are shown beyond the credit the uploader chose.
- **Limits:** JPEG or PNG, a size cap, and 5 uploads per visitor per day.
- **Chatbot:** it may show community photos as examples but never uses them to say a saree is genuine, to identify a weave from a photo, or to identify a person.
- **Removal:** requests are completed within 48 hours.
- **Storage:** stored photos cost money, so there is a cap on total storage (see [budget.md](budget.md)).
- **Legal check before launch:** photos of people, the licence wording and the uploader's declarations (India's data protection law applies to images of identifiable people).
