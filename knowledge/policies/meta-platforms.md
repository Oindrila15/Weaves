# Instagram and Facebook rules

**Last checked:** 7 October 2026 · **Review when:** before Phase 2, before any Meta integration, every 3 months, or when Meta changes its terms. Not legal advice.

## What the sources say

- **Meta's Automated Data Collection Terms** (effective 7 October 2024) prohibit bots, crawlers and scrapers without Meta's **express written permission**. Accepting the terms is not that permission. Publicly available personal data is covered. Permitted uses are limited to things like search engine results and URL previews. Meta may revoke permission, demand deletion of data and audit. [Source](https://www.facebook.com/apps/site_scraping_tos_terms.php)
- **Instagram's Terms of Use** do not allow accessing or collecting information in unauthorised ways. [Source](https://help.instagram.com/740480200552298)
- **Instagram hashtag search** (official API), as reported by search results: you need Meta's app review and the Instagram Public Content Access feature, a professional Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page, and business verification. It allows 30 unique hashtags per 7 days. Stories hashtags are not supported and results exclude personal information. [Meta's page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-facebook-login/hashtag-search). We have not confirmed the details on Meta's own page yet.
- **Not yet checked:** Instagram Business Discovery, Facebook Page public content access, and embedding of public posts. Check before using any of them.

## Weaves policy

1. **No automated collection** from Instagram or Facebook. No scraping, crawling, bots or fetching their pages with our tools.
2. A person adds a weaver's Instagram or Facebook link by hand. We store the link and the date, nothing scraped.
3. The discovery agent never opens Meta pages. If a search result points to a Meta profile, it keeps only the profile link and display name, and a person opens the profile and confirms it.
4. No images or captions are copied from Meta platforms.
5. Weavers connecting their own accounts through Meta's official login is a possible Phase 3 feature. It needs Meta app review first.
6. If a weaver asks us to remove their link, we remove it within 48 hours.
