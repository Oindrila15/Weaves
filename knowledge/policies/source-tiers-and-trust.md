# Source tiers and trust

**Last checked:** 7 October 2026 · **Review when:** a new kind of source is added, or every 6 months.

Weaves may use any publicly available source. Each source gets a tier, and the tier sets how much we trust a fact.

## Tiers

| Tier | Kind of source | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Official and primary | Government registries and portals (IP India's GI registry, the Ministry of Textiles' handlooms portal, its census and India Handloom Brand pages), official weaver cooperative or society sites |
| 2 | Scholarly and institutional | Museums, universities, national craft institutions, published research |
| 3 | Established publishing | Reputable newspapers, magazines, books, well-run craft organisations |
| 4 | Commercial | Seller, brand and retailer pages. Useful for prices, but they have a reason to sell |
| 5 | Community and social | Influencer posts, blogs, forums, user submissions |

## Rules

- **GI and official recognition** claims come only from Tier 1.
- **Prices** need two independent sources and are shown as ranges.
- **Tier 5 never stands alone.** It needs support from a higher tier, or it stays low trust.
- When sources disagree, the higher tier wins and the disagreement is shown.
- Every fact stores: source link, publisher, tier, date accessed.

## Trust level shown to visitors

- **High:** one Tier 1 source, or two or more independent Tier 2 or 3 sources that agree.
- **Medium:** one Tier 2 or 3 source, or two or more Tier 4 sources that agree.
- **Low:** anything else. Low-trust facts are held for human review before they are published.

## Freshness

Prices lose one trust level after 6 months. A weaver's profile link is rechecked after 12 months. History and technique do not expire.
