# Personal data and weaver contacts

**Last checked:** 7 October 2026 · **Review when:** before launch (legal check), or when India issues DPDP rules or guidance. Not legal advice.

## What we found

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 does not apply to personal data that the person made public themselves, or that someone made public because the law required it (Section 3(c)(ii)). The test is narrow: it depends on how the data became public, and it does not allow unlimited use. [Source](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-act-publicly-available-personal-data-46899.html/)

## Weaves policy

1. **We do not copy phone numbers, emails or personal addresses** from any public source. This decision keeps us safe whatever the exemption means in practice.
2. A weaver is listed with their name (or business name), the weaves they make, the town or district, and a link to their public profile.
3. A weaver's own contact details are shown only if the weaver gives them to us and agrees. We keep a record of that consent.
4. No automated collection of contact data. Meta's terms also forbid it for their platforms.
5. We collect as little visitor data as we can. The chatbot needs no login and chats are not tied to a person.
6. Removal requests are completed within 48 hours.
7. **Legal check before launch** on listing named individuals and on the "Know a weaver?" form.
