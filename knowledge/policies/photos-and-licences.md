# Photos and licences

**Last checked:** 7 October 2026 · **Review when:** a new photo source is added, or before launch.

## Allowed

Only these licences: **public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA.** Main source: Wikimedia Commons, where almost all files are under a free licence or in the public domain. [Reuse guide](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)

## Not allowed

- NonCommercial or NoDerivatives licences (they would block future changes to the site).
- Photos from Instagram, Facebook, Google Images, shops or news sites.
- Photos with no clear licence.

## How we record each photo

Title, author, licence name and link, source page link, date retrieved. The photo shows a credit line with the author, licence and a link.

## Rules

- **CC BY** needs credit and a licence link. **CC BY-SA** needs the same, and any altered version must stay CC BY-SA. We show photos as they are and crop only.
- Wikimedia does not guarantee that its licence information is correct, so a person checks each file's own page before use.
- If a rights holder objects, we remove the photo within 48 hours.
